# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### 🛡️ Child Safety First
- Always consider the impact on children's safety
- Prioritize security and privacy in all discussions
- Remember we're building tools to protect vulnerable users

### 🤝 Positive Behavior
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal/political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxing)
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Dismissiveness of safety concerns or accessibility needs

## 🎯 Project-Specific Guidelines

### 🔒 Security and Privacy
- Take security concerns seriously and report them immediately
- Never suggest solutions that compromise child safety
- Respect user privacy in all discussions and code
- Follow responsible disclosure for security issues

### ♿ Accessibility and Inclusion
- Consider users with disabilities in all discussions
- Ensure suggestions work for children of different ages
- Think about users with limited technical skills
- Consider cultural and linguistic diversity

### 🌱 Learning and Growth
- Welcome questions from beginners
- Provide constructive, helpful feedback
- Share knowledge and mentor others
- Celebrate learning and improvement

## 📞 Reporting Issues

### 🚨 Immediate Safety Concerns
If you see something that could immediately harm children:
- Email: kontakt@fundacja-hospicjum.org
- Mark as "URGENT: Safety Concern"

### 🐛 Code or Project Issues
- Use GitHub Issues with appropriate labels
- Be specific and provide reproduction steps
- Include screenshots if applicable

### 😔 Community Problems
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
- Email: kontakt@fundacja-hospicjum.org
- Private message to maintainers
- We will respond within 24 hours

## 🔧 Enforcement

### Consequences
- **Warning:** Private conversation about behavior
- **Temporary Suspension:** Removed from project for specified period
- **Permanent Ban:** Removed from all project spaces permanently

### Appeal Process
- Email: kontakt@fundacja-hospicjum.org
- Subject: "Code of Conduct Appeal"
- We will review within 3 business days

### Enforcement Guidelines
Our team is committed to:
- Responding to all reports within 24 hours
- Investigating reports thoroughly and fairly
- Protecting privacy of those who report issues
- Taking appropriate action based on findings

## 🌍 Cultural Considerations

### 🇵🇱 Polish Context
- Respect Polish cultural norms and values
- Consider local regulations and requirements
- Be mindful of language barriers and provide translations when possible

### 🌐 International Community
- Welcome contributors from all backgrounds
- Provide translation assistance when needed
- Consider time zones in communication
- Be patient with language differences
